## Content decisions

- PNP tutorial pp. 2–13 supports account/profile, appointment station selection, payment confirmation, one government-issued ID, appointment reference, and personal biometric capture. Standard first-time appearance only. No fixed fees or time promises are included.
- NBI How-to supports profile, branch appointment, payment record, reference/QR code, two original valid government-issued IDs, biometrics, and following release instructions. No specific release duration is promised.
- NBI Charter and How-to list different fees. LakbayG omits all fee amounts and directs users to the active portal. Do not treat the older charter's branch layout or schedule as nationally applicable.
- Taal's department page links a PDF under `/2019/03/`. Its current applicability to an existing-record copy and current office address were not confirmed. LakbayG withholds detailed steps and checklist. - PNP and NBI office selection happens in their official portals; LakbayG never selects a station or branch automatically.
