## Content decisions

- PNP tutorial supports account/profile, appointment station selection, payment confirmation, one government-issued ID, appointment reference, and personal biometric capture.
- NBI guides support profile, branch appointment, payment record, reference/QR code, two original valid government-issued IDs, biometrics, and HIT verification awareness.
- PSA, DFA Passport, PhilSys, SSS, and PhilHealth journeys reflect standard first-time adult/student registration workflows and direct users to official portals for final slot and fee confirmation.
- Taal's department page links a PDF under `/2019/03/`. Its current applicability and office counter were not confirmed, so LakbayG marks it `verified: false` and withholds detailed steps and checklist items.
- Office selection happens in official portals; LakbayG never assigns a station or branch automatically.
